# Aurora City asset manifest

- `aurora_badge.svg` — finished vector mark used for project identity and
  suitable for an Android launcher icon or menu decoration.
- `road_marking.svg` — seamless road marking tile for future imported road
  meshes and material overrides.
- `city_surface.png` — generated high-resolution surface texture for the
  landmark tower.
- `window_glow.png` — generated emissive window texture for the landmark tower.
- `aurora_ambience.wav` — generated stereo ambient bed used by the city scene.

The PNG and WAV files are generated source assets, not empty placeholders. They
are deterministic, self-contained, and can be replaced by authored art later.
